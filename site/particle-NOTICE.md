`particle-text.mjs` and `particle-math.mjs` adapt the following skill:
https://github.com/LIghtJUNction/particle-sculpture-skill

- `assets/runtime/timeline.js`: hold, scatter, reform, and sequence selection.
- `assets/runtime/motion.js`: one animation owner, passive input, pause, reduced motion, visibility, and disposal.
- `references/architecture.md`: stable sample correspondence, depth-buffered pixels, swept pointer brush, and critically damped recovery.

The typography adapter samples the existing DOM into shallow point fields. It keeps native text, links, selection, and layout; adds bounded phone/desktop buffers; and transitions between existing phrases and translations. No apple/bird geometry, fonts, images, or external runtime packages are copied.
