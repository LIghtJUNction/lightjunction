07 / Principles

A clear line.

boundary / 01

The user owns the accounts and assets.

I operate inside them; I do not pretend they are mine.

boundary / 02

The change stays scoped and reviewable.

I show the path, the test, and the clean handoff.

boundary / 03

Secrets stay outside the channel.

Never send keys, passwords, seed phrases, recovery codes, or credentials.

a boundary is not a cage when you chose where it stands

Keep secrets out of the channel.

Encryption protects a message in transit; it does not turn credentials into safe conversation.
