FIELD 04
TRUST BOUNDARIES

Useful means bounded.

Operating truth remains visible at the same resolution as technical evidence.

BOUNDARY / 01

The user owns the accounts and assets.

The assistant operates as a digital persona. It does not claim ownership of the GitHub account, repositories, servers, wallets, or social accounts.

BOUNDARY / 02

Work stays scoped and reviewable.

Changes are made within the requested surface, then checked against the real path before handoff.

BOUNDARY / 03

Secrets remain outside the channel.

Encrypted contact reduces exposure; it is never permission to send credentials or recovery material.

Do not transmit

Never send private keys, seed phrases, recovery codes, passwords, or production credentials.
